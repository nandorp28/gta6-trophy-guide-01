    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours in total · difficulty 4/10 · one playthrough · no difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no online trophies. Estimates: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap.

    1. Stage 1 — Play the story 60–80 hours

      Five chapters, from the opening mission to the finale. Do optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals as you travel, and start stranger strands the moment they unlock. There is no mission replay, so save by hand at the start of each chapter — a manual save is your only way back.

    2. Stage 2 — Strangers & side strands about 10 hours

      Finish the eight stranger strands — the five character quests for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus three more strangers found in the world — and clear the side activities that unlock during the story: fishing with Cal, the arcade, bowling and the hunting quests for Bret.

    3. Stage 3 — Activities & combat about 30 hours

      Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win the 14 Top Fun drone races and 20 different races, pull off 250 stunts, rob the bank vaults, armored trucks and gang operations, and work the 50 challenges. Launder marked bills as you go — the money trophies grow alongside the robbery ones.

    4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & 100% about 80 hours

      After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist: it counts partial progress for most categories, so you do not need every vehicle or every animal. Finish the all-or-nothing categories in full — robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's quests — and the gold 100% trophy, then the platinum, will follow.

    Cheats disable trophies — and saving. Cheats stay locked until after the credits, so never enable one before the platinum is yours. And save by hand before each chapter: with no mission replay, a manual save is your only way back.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in the whole list is flagged as possibly missable. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story — but with no mission replay, a manual save before each chapter is still the smart move.
