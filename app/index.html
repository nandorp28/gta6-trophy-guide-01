Roadmap to the platinum

About 200 hours in total · difficulty 4/10 · one playthrough · no difficulty trophies, no mission medals, no online trophies. Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap and GTA-Zone.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story, 60–80 hours. Five chapters, from the opening mission to the finale. Do optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals with binoculars as you travel, and start Stranger mission strands the moment they unlock. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so free-roam after the credits is where the cleanup happens.
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist, about 100 hours. After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The checklist is lenient — roughly 60% true completion: scan 200 of 277 vehicles, study 100 of 255 animals, and finish 70 of 80 Challenges (skip the hardest). Only a few tasks need everything: robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests.
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup, about 10 hours. Finish the eight stranger strands, the character quests for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, then mop up whatever the checklist still shows. The platinum pops when the other 54 are earned.

Cheats disable trophies — and saving. All 61 cheats stay locked until after the credits, so never enable one before the platinum is yours. And save by hand before each chapter: with no mission replay, a manual save is your only way back.

Missable trophies: just one

Only one trophy in the whole list is flagged as possibly missable. Every other trophy can be earned in free-roam after the story.

Positive Mental Attitude Bronze Missable?

Chapter 1 balcony warning: the first meditation spot is Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house, on the morning after the mission “Down to Earth”. Reports say it disappears after Chapter 1 — it is unconfirmed whether that one spot is required, but do it while you are there to be safe. There are 18 meditation spots across 10 locations in total (GTABase); the 100% checklist counts 15, so sources disagree on the number — visit all 18 and you cover both counts. Source: EGamers, GTABase.
